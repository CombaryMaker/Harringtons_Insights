WHAT THE RESULTS SHOW
  1. HX Complete is the smallest program overall on 6 of the 9 corpora: Canterbury, Calgary, Kodak, Squash Snappy,
     Maximum Compression and Lukas. kanzi -l 9 wins Silesia (1.80%), enwik8 (3.44%) and Large Canterbury (1.18%).
  2. Images and medical data are its strongest ground: Kodak photos 15.63% smaller than kanzi -l 9, Lukas medical
     X-rays 15.73% smaller than the best rival (bzip3) and 17.80% smaller than kanzi -l 9.
  3. Its weak ground is machine code and large plain text (Silesia program files, enwik8, bible.txt).
  4. HX Fast is first in its speed race on Silesia and Lukas, second on Maximum Compression and Large Canterbury.
  5. Speed tiers: Harrington's compression concepts won most tiers on 7 of the 9 corpora.
  6. HX Complete compresses far more slowly than the rivals (minutes where kanzi takes seconds); decoding is fast.

NEWER HX VERSIONS (not used in the runs above)
  HX 1.1.0 (10 Nov): block mode, memory limit, threads flag, stdin/stdout; same output as 1.0.1 without the new flags.
  HX 1.1.1 / 1.0.2 (10 Nov): mode context route for dialogue-rich books and Wikipedia-style text (0.18%-0.71% smaller
  on such text in testing); identical output on other files.
