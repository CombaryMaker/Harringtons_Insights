WHAT THE FOUR RUNS SHOW
  1. Size: HX Complete is the smallest overall on three of four corpora (Canterbury, Calgary, Kodak) and over all four
     together (2.21% smaller than kanzi -l 9). It was smallest on 51 of the 65 files.
  2. Images and structured data are its strongest ground: Kodak 15.63% smaller than kanzi -l 9 and smallest on all 24
     photos; kennedy.xls 62.7% and the fax image (ptt5 / pic) 31.0% smaller than the best rival; Silesia mr 16.2%,
     sao 11.5%, x-ray 3.0%.
  3. Its one consistent weakness is machine code: Silesia ooffice (kanzi 15.0% smaller), samba (14.4%), mozilla (6.4%),
     Calgary obj2 (9.8%) and obj1 (6.7%). These files decide Silesia, which kanzi -l 9 wins by 1.83%.
  4. Decode: HX Complete decodes 4.9x faster than kanzi -l 9 on Silesia and 7.5x faster on Kodak, and about the same on
     the two small corpora. Compression is far slower (26 to 88 times kanzi -l 9).
  5. HX Fast: smallest in its race on Silesia; 7th and 10th on the two small corpora, where kanzi -l 7, BSC, xz and
     7-Zip were smaller and times are mostly program start-up.
  6. Speed tiers: on every corpus the slow tiers are won by HX Complete (except Silesia's tiers 6-10, kanzi -l 9);
     the middle-fast tiers are the contested ground (BSC best, kanzi -l 7, bzip3).
  7. On the two small corpora LZ4 decodes the whole set in about 0.3 s, so their speed tiers mostly measure program
     start-up; their sizes are fully valid.
