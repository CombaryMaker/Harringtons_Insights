NOTES
H-Deflate was previously released as Patent Pending under research-only terms. After a prior-art review on
7 November 2026 it is released under the MIT License and no patent claim is made on it. Prior work it relates to:
precomp, preflate and reflate (deflate recompression); precomp2 (DarkSky, Apache-2.0) and packPNG, which also
unfilter PNG rows to pixels for an image codec. H-Deflate is written from RFC 1951 and contains no code from zlib,
precomp, preflate, precomp2, packPNG or 7-Zip. The fast inflater uses long-standing decoder techniques (as in
zlib-turbo and libdeflate). The optional zcheck test mode links the system zlib (zlib licence) only to compare
results. HX, which can use H-Deflate's output, remains Patent Pending under its own license (HX/LICENSE.txt).
