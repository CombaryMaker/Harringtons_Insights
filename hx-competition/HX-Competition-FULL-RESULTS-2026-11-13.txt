2. WHAT THE RESULTS SHOW
  - HX Complete is the smallest program overall on 10 of the 15 corpora: Canterbury, Calgary, Kodak, Squash Snappy,
    Maximum Compression, DNA and all four Lukas sets. Rivals win Silesia (kanzi -l 9, 1.80%), enwik8 (kanzi -l 9, 3.44%),
    Large Canterbury (kanzi -l 9, 1.18%), UIQ2 (BSC, 9 bytes) and Canterbury artificial (Brotli, 51 bytes).
  - Over all 15 corpora (1.6 GB) HX Complete is 5.11% smaller than kanzi -l 9.
  - Images and medical data are its strongest ground: Lukas 2D 16-bit TIF 16.69% and DICOM 15.73% smaller than the best
    rival, Kodak 15.63%, Lukas 8-bit 6.98%, Lukas 3D 3.65%.
  - Its weak ground is machine code and large plain text (Silesia program files, enwik8, bible.txt).
  - HX Fast is first in its speed race on Silesia, DNA, Lukas 2D 16-bit DICOM, Lukas 8-bit and Lukas 3D; on Lukas 3D it is
    smaller than every rival while decoding faster than LZ4 -1.
  - Speed tiers: Harrington's compression concepts won most tiers on 11 of the 15 corpora.
  - HX Complete compresses far more slowly than the rivals (minutes to hours where kanzi takes seconds); decoding is fast
    except where its context-mixing route wins (DNA: 64 s against Brotli's 2.6 s).
