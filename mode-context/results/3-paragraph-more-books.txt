FINDINGS
  - The control reshuffle (gc) made every book larger with every compressor, so the gains come from grouping by
    dialogue, not from reordering as such.
  - Context-mixing compressors gain from dialogue grouping: HCM on 7 of 9 novels (best Gatsby -0.57%, Huck Finn -0.48%,
    Pride and Prejudice -0.43%), the test model on all 9. xz and HX Fast gain on some novels and lose on others;
    bzip2 mostly loses. Nonfiction: no effect, as expected (almost no dialogue).
  - g3 (one-line paragraphs as a third group) is about the same as g2; g4 (splitting dialogue by turn) is worse.
  - Grouping plus the free paragraph guessers (g2 + A) beat the control on all 11 books; fiction 0.12% to 0.74%.
  - The speaker-turn guesser (D) is worse than A: drop it.
