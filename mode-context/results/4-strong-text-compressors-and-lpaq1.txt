FINDINGS
  - The context-mixing tools gain from dialogue grouping: kanzi -l 9 on 6 of 9 novels (best Gatsby -0.73%, Pride and
    Prejudice -0.57%, Huck Finn -0.52%), lpaq1 on 7 of 9. The control reshuffle hurts every tool on every book.
  - BSC loses slightly on every book (block sorting already gathers similar contexts; the note is pure cost).
    PPMd is mixed (helps 3 novels).
  - Inside lpaq1 the paragraph context beats an equal plain context on 8 of 9 novels (up to 0.10%); with grouping,
    up to 0.69% better than the equal-size control. No effect on nonfiction.
  - Small, steady gains on dialogue-rich fiction; mostly between a tenth and three-quarters of a percent.
