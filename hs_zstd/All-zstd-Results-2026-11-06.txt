ALL zstd RESULTS - Michael Hugh Harrington - 6 November 2026
Every recorded result involving zstd: stock zstd, pzstd, hs_zstd (Harrington's Sets, multi-core), zstd-hi (tuned
encoder, stock decoder), record-delta front ends, and HX routes that use zstd. Every result was decompressed and
checked byte for byte. Extracted from All-Software-Test-Results-2026-11-06.txt (full list on GitHub, HX/results).

COLUMNS: tool | mark | compressed bytes | ratio (original / compressed) | compress s | compress MB/s |
         decompress s | decompress MB/s.   n/r = not recorded.   ~ = derived from a recorded ratio.
MARKS:   * = our own tool (hs_zstd, HX)   m = modified encoder (zstd-hi; stock zstd decoder)
         r = record-delta front end / mutant   (no mark) = stock zstd or pzstd

SUMMARY
- Multi-core (hs_zstd): decoding averaged 2.14x faster than zstd and 1.40x faster than pzstd over the 2 GB, 3.2 GB,
  4 GB and 10 GB tests (best: 10 GB, 2.99x vs zstd, 1.72x vs pzstd), at nearly the same ratio (4 GB Silesia
  3.171x vs zstd 3.184x).
- Single-core (zstd-hi): small no-pain size gains (Canterbury -1 4.09x -> 4.14x, -3 4.43 -> 4.46, -9 4.71 -> 4.75,
  -19 5.44 -> 5.45); compression 2x faster on the counties export (72.9 s -> 36.3 s).
- Record delta + zstd: large gains on tabular data (kennedy.xls zstd -22 15.89x -> 39.01x), small on mixed data
  (Silesia zstd -19 4.01x -> 4.03x).
- Multi-core results in detail (raw logs on GitHub, hs_zstd/results-*.txt):
    4 GB Silesia, 12 threads: zstd 3.73 s, pzstd 2.85 s, hs_zstd 4 MB blocks 2.27 s (decode median)
    10 GB:  zstd 11.95 s, pzstd 6.87 s, hs_zstd 4.00 s      2 GB: zstd 1.67 s, pzstd 0.94 s, hs_zstd 0.63 s
    3.2 GB mixed public tar: zstd 2.23 s, pzstd 1.92 s, hs_zstd 1.75 s; Sets ratio 4.093x vs plain blocks 4.076x
    Core scaling (4 GB, hs_zstd 4 MB): 1 core 7.52 s, 2: 4.87, 4: 3.24, 6: 2.71, 8: 2.55, 12 threads: 2.43 s
