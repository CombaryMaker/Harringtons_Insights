FACEBOOK POST TEXT (paste as the post; the image captions are in captions.txt, one per picture)

I have been working on data compression for a while now, and these charts put the best result from every tool I have built or modified beside the standard tools that the world uses every day, measured on the same benchmarks. Some of these are my own compressors, like HCM and the Harrington Exchange, some are standard tools whose encoders I tuned so that the normal decoder still reads the files, and some are standard tools with my own front ends added, like record detection. On the Canterbury corpus my best is smaller than ZPAQ at its strongest setting and decodes more than three times faster, on server logs and data exports my tuned xz is the smallest of every tool tested, my PDF row filter beats qpdf, my FLAC tuning is smaller and faster for speech, and on a real spreadsheet and a real medical x-ray my changes make standard tools far smaller and faster at the same time. Where I am still behind on size, such as against ZPAQ on the full Silesia corpus, my tools decode several times faster. Every result was decompressed and checked byte for byte, and the code, the raw numbers and the book, Harrington's Insights, are open on GitHub at https://github.com/CombaryMaker/Harringtons_Insights for anyone who wants to check the work.
