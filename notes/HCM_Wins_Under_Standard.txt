======================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS FILE
======================================================================
Sizes: "1,000,000 bits down to X" (per-million), with exact bytes where kept.
Every result listed decoded back to the original exactly (byte for byte).

Classes:
  Original = your code and methods (your decoder)
  Mutated  = their engine underneath, but needs a new decoder
  Modified = their format; the stock decoder reads it unchanged

Standards:
  No-Pain = smaller with no slower decode, faster with no larger size, or both
  BGS-1   = 10% smaller or 1.25x faster decode; other metric may regress up to 5%
  BGS-2   = 25% smaller or 2x faster decode; other metric may regress up to 15%
  BGS-3   = 50% smaller or 10x faster decode; no fixed regression limit
            (a Tier BGS-3 also needs no tested rival in its tier to dominate it)
  Decode times within 3% count as equal.

Award record format: Award / Coverage / Corpus or file
  All awards below are Baseline awards (beat one named compressor setting).
  No Tier awards yet: no tier's strongest rivals have been tested side by side.

Tiers (decode multiple M = decode time / lz4 decode time, same machine):
  1 Speedsters        M up to 2
  2 Mainstream        M over 2, up to 10
  3 Medium Sluggers   M over 10, up to 100
  4 Heavy             M over 100, up to 3,000
  5 Insane            M over 3,000
  All tiers below are PROVISIONAL: set from the parent compressor's measured
  multiple on Silesia (lz4 1.0, zstd -1 2.7, zstd -3 3.0, gzip -1 12.8,
  xz -0 61.0). Each pipeline's own M is NOT measured yet. HCM is an estimate.

Test files:
  kennedy.xls  Canterbury corpus, Excel spreadsheet, 1,029,744 bytes, record length 13
  ptt5         Canterbury corpus, fax image, 513,216 bytes, record length 216
  Canterbury   all 11 Canterbury corpus files
  x-ray        Silesia corpus, medical X-ray image, 8,474,240 bytes, record length 2
  mr           Silesia corpus, MRI image, 9,970,564 bytes, record length 2
