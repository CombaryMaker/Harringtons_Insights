FINDINGS
- HCM beats every LZ/BWT codec in the run (lzma, xz, bsc, brotli, zstd, bzip3) on size, but not the two
  context-mixing codecs zpaq 5 and kanzi 9; kanzi 9 is both smaller and faster than HCM.
- NOTABLE: HCM in Harrington's Sets mode (2 cores) was SMALLER than single-stream HCM fast (43,189,460 vs
  43,795,425) and 2.26x faster to compress, 1.82x faster to decode - the same pattern as the industrial data test.
- hs_zstd Sets vs plain blocks: Sets 48,660 bytes smaller (0.1%); both about 1% larger than single-stream zstd 19.
- xz-hi: run separately (its settings search takes over 1.5 hours on a 212 MB file); see results-xzhi.txt.
