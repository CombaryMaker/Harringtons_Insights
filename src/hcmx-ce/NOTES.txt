HCMX-CE: CANDIDATE ESTIMATOR WITHOUT THE TERNARY FLAG - 30 October 2026
Michael Hugh Harrington. Mutant of hcmx (HCM v3.3 engine + ternary pointer). Test of Andrew Goodwin's point that
the pointer's gain comes from the refinement mixer, not the ternary flag.

WHAT CHANGED (px.inc, enabled with HCMX_CE=1 for both compress and decompress; test build only)
The "is it the candidate?" flag is removed. Every byte is coded bit by bit as normal. While the bits still follow
the candidate's path, a small 4-input mixer (model chance, an escape cell keyed by candidate type, match length,
last two candidate outcomes and bit position, and a cell keyed by the previous 2 bytes, the candidate and bit)
re-estimates the chance that the next bit equals the candidate's bit. Same candidates as the pointer.

RESULTS, Canterbury (2,810,784 bytes), fast mode, binary files in binary mode, every file restored exactly.
Decode = sum of per-file medians of 5 runs (includes program start-up; 2-core cloud machine).
                         bytes      1,000,000 bits down to   compress   decode
  v3.3, no pointer       353,494    125,763                   4.41 s     2.49 s
  ternary pointer        351,302    124,984                   5.52 s     2.89 s
  candidate estimator    351,848    125,178                   5.55 s     3.08 s

OBSERVATIONS
- Without any ternary flag, the estimator keeps about 75% of the pointer's size gain (1,646 of 2,192 bytes saved
  against v3.3) - so most of the gain is indeed secondary estimation, as Andrew said.
- It is NOT faster. It decodes about 7% slower than the pointer and 24% slower than v3.3. The pointer is faster
  because on a correct guess it decodes one flag instead of eight bits; the estimator always decodes eight bits.
  So the flag's real value is speed on hits (an ordinary escape flag), not compression.
- The estimator wins on cp.html, grammar.lsp and xargs.1, ties elsewhere, and loses on ptt5.
- Conclusion: neither version needs Combary or ternary framing. The pointer is best described as an escape flag
  with a secondary estimator; the flag-free estimator is a simpler option that is smaller than v3.3 but slower.
