HOW TO USE
1. Double-click HX-Compressor.exe.
2. Drag files or folders into the window, or press "Add files..." or "Add folder...".
3. Choose a format under "Save as" and, for HX, choose HX Fast or HX Complete.
4. Press Compress. The archive is saved next to the first file unless you pick another folder.
5. To unpack anything, press "Open archive and extract..." and choose where it should go.
Every archive is opened again and checked against your files before the app reports success
(you can switch the check off).

THE FORMATS
  HX (.hx)          Our own format. HX Fast is quick and very fast to open; HX Complete makes the smallest files.
                    Opening an .hx file needs HX (this app, or hx.exe).
  ZIP, 7z, tar.gz, tar.xz   Standard archives that other programs open. Made with 7-Zip.
  zstd, Brotli, kanzi       Fast or strong general compressors (several files are put in a .tar first).
  FLAC, WavPack             For WAV audio, lossless.
  WebP lossless, JPEG XL    For images, lossless. JPEG XL also repacks JPEG photos smaller without any loss.
The extra six formats were chosen from the tools that won the most categories in our 290-category results.

THE TOOLS FOLDER
hx.exe is included. The other formats use the official programs, which go in the "tools" folder beside the app
(see TOOLS.txt). If 7-Zip is installed in the usual place, the app finds it by itself. A format whose program is
missing is marked "not installed" in the list.
